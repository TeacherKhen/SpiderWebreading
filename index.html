Spiderweb Reading

An Interactive Phonics Activity

Welcome, little readers! Follow the spiderweb, connect the letters, and discover how letters come together to make words.

Letter Sounds Connect Letters Read Words

How to Play

1

Look at the Letters

Find the colorful letters on the spiderweb. Think about the word you want to make.

2

Tap the Letters

Choose the letters one by one in the correct order. Watch the web connect your letters!

3

Say the Sounds

Say each letter sound, blend the sounds together, and read the word aloud.

4

Try Again!

Tap Clear Path to start a new word. Keep practicing and have fun!

Remember!

You can tap a letter you already selected to go back to it. Your teacher can customize the letters and upload classroom pictures in Teacher Settings.

Let's Read Together!
1

Look at the Letters

Look carefully at the colorful letters on the spiderweb. Think of a word you want to build.

2

Tap to Connect

Tap the letters one by one in the correct order. Watch the web connect as you build your word!

3

Read Your Word

Look at the letters you connected. Say each sound, blend the sounds, and read the word aloud.

4

Try Another Word

Tap Clear Path to start again. Build a new word and keep practicing!
